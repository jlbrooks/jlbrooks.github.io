Way-too-late post about our trip to Keuka lake this summer. This time was just Rory and I as Kaitlin was not able to travel - she was missed by all but Rory had a wonderful time.

Few things bring me as much joy as seeing Rory discover his love of Keuka lake and time spent with family there. He is much more capable now and took full advantage of his motor skills and attentive grandparents to make the most of our ten day visit.

We started off with a too-short overlap with my cousins from Florida, Rory got to meet his cousin Brooks for the first time. They had a blast and both pushed each other to try new things, such fun!
