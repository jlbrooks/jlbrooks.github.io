## What I'm playing
[The Blood of Dawnwalker](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3751260/The_Blood_of_Dawnwalker/) is awesome. It's been a while since I played a game in this genre, and this one has me hooked. I think it's a combination of:
- The unique setting and lore (werewolf-infused vampires, rad)
- The time system and day/night cycle keeps things moving
- The combat feels great

I think they could have leaned even harder into the time mechanics, it's still too forgiving. It's perfect for this kind of game, though, adding some structure and propulsion to what could be an open world laundry-list of activities.
