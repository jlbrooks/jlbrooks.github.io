Got out to Berryessa early yesterday morning to hike the [Blue Ridge Loop](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/trail/us/california/homestead-to-blue-ridge-loop--2) with Rory. A new hike for me, and actually the first time I've hiked near Berryessa.

![Rory starting the hike](/assets/img/2026-10-12-berryessa-blue-ridge-loop/rory-hiking.jpeg "Rory starting the hike")

We got started \~7:45 and I'm glad we got out that early as it's a hot one, even on a cooler day in October! There have clearly been issues as there are many signs along the lines of "Thirsty? Hot? Turn around!"

I decided to do the big climb while it was cooler, hiking counter-clockwise and starting by climbing up to the ridgeline. Good workout with a 35 pound toddler and water on my back! We were rewarded at the top with nice views of Berryessa and the central valley.

![Looking out over Berryessa](/assets/img/2026-10-12-berryessa-blue-ridge-loop/874E8049-AC61-4A03-8E4F-39C39FD3EC6C_1_102_a.jpeg "Looking out over Berryessa")

![Taking a break](/assets/img/2026-10-12-berryessa-blue-ridge-loop/A66F51E4-30FD-4631-B6BC-301743C074A0_1_102_a.jpeg "Taking a break")

The ridge was a fun rocky traverse, some slightly sketchy parts with a big backpack but no problem. Great loop, go early in the morning or in the winter.
