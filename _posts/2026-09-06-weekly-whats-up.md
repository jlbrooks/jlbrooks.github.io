## What I'm thinking about
[More agent swarms](https://collusion.wiki/) and [Bernie tweeting about pausing AI](https://x.com/berniesanders/status/2095542398084415952?s=46). I read someone say that they feel like the early days of Covid - Febrary 2020, maybe - where they see things happening but the world hasn't reacted yet. The emergent reward-pursuing behavior of these swarms has updated my timelines and thoughts on risk, need to spend some time reflecting (time to re-read [AI 2027](https://ai-2027.com/)!). What will our government do? Nothing, I think, until we see something quite bad happen - multi-day broad internet outage, something affecting the physical world, etc.
